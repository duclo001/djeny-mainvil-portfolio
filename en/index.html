Frequently Asked Questions

What was your academic and professional background, and how has it evolved to date?

My journey began in Les Cayes, Haiti, where I completed my secondary studies at Externat Saint Joseph. Subsequently, I obtained a bachelor's degree in economics with distinction from Quisqueya University in Port-au-Prince, from 2016 to 2020. Eager to deepen my knowledge, I then pursued a master's degree in economics at the University of Montreal, Canada, which I obtained in 2021. On the professional level, I developed expertise in strategic and economic analysis by holding key positions in different sectors. As a business advisor at the Canadian Federation of Independent Business (CFIB), I conducted in-depth research and analyzed data to support the decisions of SMEs, facilitating the development of public policies in their favor. With this experience, I now specialize in business intelligence, with the goal of using my analytical skills to help businesses optimize their performance and maximize their profitability. Do economics studies offer promising prospects in Canada?

Yes, studying economics can offer promising prospects in Canada, but it depends on your career goals and interests. Canada offers a variety of economics programs that are tailored to different profiles. For example, if you are passionate about economic policy analysis or theoretical aspects of economics, a Master of Science in Economics may be a good fit for you. If you prefer a more applied approach, programs in applied economics or development economics may be more in line with your aspirations. It is essential to choose a program based on your passions and the direction you want to take your career.

What advice would you give to a young graduate wishing to continue their studies in these fields?

I would advise a recent graduate to stay resilient and gain practical experience alongside their studies. It is equally important to build a strong professional network and stay up to date on current economic trends. Before choosing a program, carefully review the courses offered and find out about the career opportunities it can lead to. This will help you make an informed choice and avoid disappointments when entering the job market. In addition, specializing in fields such as artificial intelligence can significantly enrich your profile.

What is the first step in starting the college admissions process, and how do you choose the institution that best fits your needs and budget?

The first step in beginning the admissions process is to feel confident in your decision and apply. Once you have done this, it is crucial to plan your path by carefully preparing your application, researching scholarships and preparing for required tests. Choosing a university depends on several criteria: the reputation of the institution, the programs offered, research and internship opportunities, but also your budget. What is the difference between an economist and a business analyst?

An economist focuses primarily on analyzing economic data and developing recommendations based on that analysis. Their work often involves studying large-scale economic trends, both local and global, as well as assessing the impact of economic policies. In contrast, a business analyst focuses on analyzing a company's internal data to improve processes, optimize performance, and support strategic decision-making.

Can you describe your experience in education and student counseling?

My experience in education and student counseling is marked by perseverance. I obtained my bachelor's degree in economics at Université Quisqueya despite the political crises in Haiti, then pursued a master's degree at Université de Montréal, partly remotely due to COVID-19 restrictions and immigration procedures. Upon arriving in Canada, I had to adapt to a new education system while juggling work and administrative formalities. Today, in addition to my professional experience, I am involved as a volunteer in the HEC Montréal mentoring program, where I support new students in their integration. This experience fuels my commitment to supporting others and facilitating their academic transition.
